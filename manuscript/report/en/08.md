The A–φ model recovers current using {{eqref:E07}} from saved A and actual gradients of φ. The three cylinder meshes contain 1683, 3672, and 9108 nodes; results appear in {{figref:aphi_gates}}. Radial J calibration meets its target on the finest mesh, while φ and gauge residuals fail on all three; the two coarser meshes also fail the J target. Manufactured-solution checks support correspondence between the tested equations and implementation, while physical cylinder calibration reveals field–potential consistency questions requiring further research.

{{figref:aphi_preserved}} uses 128 explicitly identified nodal samples to display A, φ, J and the actual φ gradient at those locations. The reproduction materials retain boundary, scaling, derivative and postprocessing diagnostics, together with failure causes for direct factorization, out-of-core and iterative routes. These records help distinguish implementation problems from discretization errors. The current cylinder results have not passed physical acceptance.
