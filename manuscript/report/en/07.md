The case is a homogeneous copper cuboid of 1×1×2 mm at 200 kHz, with imposed Bz=1 mT RMS and zero net transport. Three-component current is represented by face-flux degrees of freedom, with separate continuity, port-constraint, and imposed-field power checks. At quadrature q=14, meshes of 8192, 16000, and 27648 cells give losses of 0.927463, 0.933539, and 0.936927 mW. Consecutive refinement changes are 0.655090% and 0.362916%, below the 1% target. Increasing the finest case to q=18 changes loss and complex face flux relatively by 1.55×10⁻⁸ and 1.39×10⁻⁸, respectively, below the 0.1% quadrature target; see {{figref:rt0_spatial}}.

Sections and vectors in {{figref:rt0_sections}} and {{figref:rt0_vectors}} are reconstructed from saved face fluxes. Cell-center current is obtained by averaging opposite-face fluxes and dividing by face area. Cell-average loss density uses the full local RT0 overlap integral and therefore includes within-cell variation. Arrows show the real current at zero phase with one common length scale; color represents the complex RMS norm. Section locations and vector samples follow the figure annotations, while volume integration uses all cells.

Each mesh records 35 GMRES iterations and meets the 10⁻¹¹ true-residual target. Spatial and quadrature comparisons jointly support the fixed-cuboid imposed-field result. Applicability has yet to extend to curved Litz masks, independently validated boundary fluxes, and actual transport terminals. The original q=18 summary's missing metrics and failure status, CSV-based postprocessing acceptance, and resource records for dense and matrix-free implementations are retained separately to preserve the relationship between solution and interpretation.
