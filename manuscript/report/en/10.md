## CPU/GPU numerical comparison and computational scale

<!-- id: 10.compute.p1 -->
With identical model, discretization, and integration settings, the 5024-degree-of-freedom CPU/GPU partial-inductance matrices differ relatively by 7.48×10⁻¹⁶ and their complex currents by 7.78×10⁻¹⁴. The 1264-degree-of-freedom case also meets the 10⁻¹⁰ comparison threshold, as shown in {{figref:gpu_equivalence}}. These results establish numerical agreement between the two implemented algorithms for the compared matrices and port solutions. Performance comparisons additionally require matching timing boundaries; stage times and resource samples are retained, while physical conclusions rely on the model checks described above.

<!-- id: 10.compute.p2 -->
{{figref:resource_memory}} shows sampled memory usage for identifiable owned process trees; short peaks between samples may exceed the displayed curve. At larger scales, the 9856-degree-of-freedom case retains recoverable partial currents and related results, while its complete matrix, common voltage and full-field coverage are itemized in the data inventory. Cases with 20108 or more degrees of freedom record resource requirements and termination boundaries. These records inform the cost of further computation; complete numerical verification rests on cases with the corresponding acceptance data.
