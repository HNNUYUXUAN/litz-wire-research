Separate integration identifies dissipation in the bundle and return. The common axial terminal field is {{metric:coax_voltage_re}}+j({{metric:coax_voltage_im}}) V/m. ## Length and integration sensitivity of longitudinal PEEC

<!-- id: 03.peec_results.p1 -->
For the 1264-degree-of-freedom model, resistances at 0.16, 0.32, and 0.64 m are 19.8992, 19.9586, and 19.9885 mΩ per axial metre. Consecutive length changes are 0.298640% and 0.149482%. The 5024-degree-of-freedom model changes by 0.148730% from 0.32 to 0.64 m, as shown in {{figref:peec_length}}. Resistance changes decrease with increasing length, and all tested increments are below 1%. The sequence characterizes sensitivity over the 0.16–0.64 m interval.

<!-- id: 03.peec_results.p2 -->
At 5024 degrees of freedom, Lz=0.64 m, mutual order q=4, and self-integration settings 12/10, Rac={{metric:peec_rac}} mΩ per axial metre, Rac/Rdc=3.64472, and loss is 8.00510 W per axial metre. Resistance differs by 0.044582% from the equal-area Bessel reference for this discretization. {{figref:peec_cross_section}} reconstructs the cross-sectional current distribution from saved filament currents and square cells, showing surface concentration within the conductor. These degrees of freedom discretize a continuous conductor; their current variation has a different meaning from current sharing among insulated strands.

## CPU/GPU numerical comparison and computational scale

<!-- id: 03.compute.p1 -->
With identical model, discretization, and integration settings, the 5024-degree-of-freedom CPU/GPU partial-inductance matrices differ relatively by 7.48×10⁻¹⁶ and their complex currents by 7.78×10⁻¹⁴. The 1264-degree-of-freedom case also meets the 10⁻¹⁰ comparison threshold, as shown in {{figref:gpu_equivalence}}. These results establish numerical agreement between the two implemented algorithms for the compared matrices and port solutions. Performance comparisons additionally require matching timing boundaries; stage times and resource samples are retained, while physical conclusions rely on the model checks described above.

<!-- id: 03.compute.p2 -->
{{figref:resource_memory}} shows sampled memory usage for identifiable owned process trees; short peaks between samples may exceed the displayed curve. At larger scales, the 9856-degree-of-freedom case retains recoverable partial currents and related results, while its complete matrix, common voltage and full-field coverage are itemized in the data inventory. Cases with 20108 or more degrees of freedom record resource requirements and termination boundaries. These records inform the cost of further computation; complete numerical verification rests on cases with the corresponding acceptance data.
