# Litz Wire Research v1.0.0-rc.1 — historical research evidence

This release preserves the original research source and evidence. Its existing tag remains at [`29bfaef3cb4e4f0016b4567edc501f34c50542d7`](https://github.com/HNNUYUXUAN/litz-wire-research/commit/29bfaef3cb4e4f0016b4567edc501f34c50542d7).

The current [dataset catalog](https://github.com/HNNUYUXUAN/litz-wire-research/blob/main/data/catalog.json) selects 13 datasets and 65 archive parts from this release: analytical calibration, two-dimensional FEM, common-terminal circuits, geometry, PEEC, RT0, A–φ diagnostics, Ansys and GPU records, publication inputs, historical editions, shared objects and research records. Manifests retain the original paths, byte counts, hashes and source-version identities.

The [archived manuscript index](https://github.com/HNNUYUXUAN/litz-wire-research/blob/main/provenance/editorial/archive/current-edition-rc1.json) identifies the historical `publication_rc1_20261007_01` set: 52 document entries, each with a Notebook and two PDF layouts. These are the archived full and chapter editions. The current six complete manuscripts, eighteen PDFs and six Notebooks are indexed in [Publications](https://github.com/HNNUYUXUAN/litz-wire-research/blob/main/publications/README.md) and distributed through [rc.2](https://github.com/HNNUYUXUAN/litz-wire-research/releases/tag/v1.0.0-rc.2).

Use the maintained source from [rc.3](https://github.com/HNNUYUXUAN/litz-wire-research/releases/tag/v1.0.0-rc.3) for credential-free selective downloads and current source checks. Follow the [dataset guide](https://github.com/HNNUYUXUAN/litz-wire-research/blob/main/docs/datasets.md); its catalog addresses this release's preserved attachments. The [distribution record](https://github.com/HNNUYUXUAN/litz-wire-research/blob/main/docs/open-source-readiness.md) records public access and download verification.

Scientific results retain their recorded model, excitation, mesh and acceptance conditions. The saved three-dimensional A–φ cylinder diagnostics include unsuccessful scalar-potential and gauge checks. Spatial-refinement results are interpreted for the specific imposed-field or transport case and its recorded mesh sequence. Publishing this evidence preserves each original run status and validation record.
